Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

CryptoPilot AI provides market-data, technical-analysis and educational software. This policy explains the information we may process when you use the service.

Information we collect

  • Account information such as email address and encrypted password data when you register.
  • Technical information needed for security, rate limiting and basic traffic analytics.
  • Market-analysis requests and settings needed to provide the requested features.
  • Payment transaction identifiers submitted for Premium verification. We do not ask for private keys or wallet seed phrases.

How we use information

We use information to operate the service, authenticate accounts, prevent abuse, provide analytics, process Premium requests and improve reliability.

Analytics

Basic page-view analytics may use a privacy-oriented hashed visitor identifier derived from request information. We do not sell personal information.

Third parties

Market data may be obtained from external providers. If AI chart analysis is configured, uploaded chart images may be sent to the configured AI provider solely to generate the requested analysis.

Security

Passwords are hashed. Sessions use secure HTTP cookies in production. Never submit a seed phrase, private key, exchange secret or full payment-card number.

Data retention

Account, security and transaction records may be retained while needed to operate the service or comply with applicable obligations.

Your choices

You may stop using the service at any time. For account or privacy requests, contact the service operator through the contact method published with the service.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the product evolves. The date above indicates the latest revision.
