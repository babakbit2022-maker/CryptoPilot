How to read this

We keep the language simple so you do not need trading experience.
🟢 Large buyingLarge trades were more often on the buy side. This can show stronger demand, but it does not guarantee that price will rise.
🔴 Large sellingLarge trades were more often on the sell side. This can show stronger selling pressure, but it does not guarantee a drop.
🟡 UnclearTransfers or trades alone are not enough to prove that a whale is accumulating or selling. CryptoPilot marks the situation as unclear when evidence is weak.
Important: a large exchange transfer is not proof of a whale buying or selling. It may also be an internal exchange move, custody transfer, market-maker activity, or another operational transaction. When the evidence is not strong enough, CryptoPilot says “Unconfirmed” instead of guessing.
